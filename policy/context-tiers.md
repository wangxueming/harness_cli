# Context tiers

| Tier | When loaded | Content |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **T1** | Every session | `AGENTS.md`, `policy/never-do.md` |
| **T2** | Domain / subagent | `policy/domains/*`, `souls/<role>/SOUL.md`, mission |
| **T3** | On demand | `docs/exec-plans/`, `docs/architecture/`, design docs |

Keep `AGENTS.md` under ~100 lines. Do not duplicate T3 in T1.
